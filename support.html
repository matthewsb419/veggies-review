Support

Veggies First is a to-do list that locks your dessert apps until the veggies are done. Here is where to get help.

Start with Help in the app

Tap the ? at the top of any tab. Every part of the app has a short guide with pictures, and a button that opens the setting it talks about.

Something is broken

Setup → Report a bug sends us what happened, with a screenshot or a screen recording if you add one. We reply in the app, under Setup → My tickets.

  • A dessert app won't lock or won't open: check that Screen Time is allowed for Veggies First in the iPhone's Settings, then open Veggies First once so it can catch up.
  • Steps aren't counting: allow Steps and Walking + Running Distance for Veggies First in the Health app, under Sharing.

Your account and your data

Setup → You has Sign out and Delete account. What the app keeps, and where, is on the privacy page. The app is licensed under Apple's standard terms of use.
